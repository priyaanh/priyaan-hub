You

Your name goes at the top of the hub, on printed worksheets and on the piano practice log. An email address is optional, and is printed under your name on a sheet you hand in. Your school year decides which grade Make Practice opens on.

Put in your own details, not anyone else’s. Everything here is saved in this browser and nowhere else. There is no account and no server behind this site, so nothing you type can reach another person. Your email address is left out of the backup file and out of sync on purpose, so even a copy of this hub that you hand to someone cannot carry it.
