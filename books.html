The CPM books

Opened from CPM, not copied here

The Core Connections books belong to CPM, so they are not kept in this hub and never will be. Everything below opens on cpm.org, where CPM publishes it: the homework help pages, which carry every problem in the book with hints and answers, and the chapter guides, which explain each idea again and give extra practice with worked solutions. Signing in to the eBook needs the class account from school.
